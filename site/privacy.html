Privacy policy

Pheasant Couch Productions · Effective October 16, 2026

Who this is from

Pheasant Couch Productions is the name Zakk M.G. makes and publishes games under. “We” on this page means Zakk M.G. This policy covers the website at pheasantcouch.com and our games: Pew Pew Pest Control.

The short version

We don’t collect personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking cookies and no advertising trackers, on this site or in our games.

Our games don’t collect personal information. This website shows ads served by Google, and Google uses cookies to do that. The details are under “Ads on this website” below.

In our games

  • Saves stay on your device. Progress, settings and achievements are stored on the device you play on: in the browser’s local storage for the web version, and in the app’s own storage on desktop and mobile. They never leave that device, and they’re deleted when you uninstall the game or clear the browser’s site data.
  • Nothing phoning home. The games contain no advertising, no in-app purchases, no third-party analytics and no crash reporting.
  • Steam. The Steam version uses Steamworks to unlock achievements on your Steam profile. Valve handles that exchange under the Steam Privacy Policy. The game itself doesn’t receive or keep your Steam account details.
  • App stores. Downloading a game from the App Store or Google Play is covered by Apple’s and Google’s own privacy policies.

On this website

  • No cookies or analytics of our own. The site itself doesn’t set cookies or measure visitors. Google’s advertising cookies are covered below.
  • Server logs. Like any website, the company that hosts this one records basic technical details of each visit, such as IP address, browser type and the page requested, to keep the site running and secure. It may also use a strictly necessary security cookie to keep bots out. We don’t use any of this to identify anyone.
  • Email. If you write to , we use your message and address to reply, and for nothing else. We don’t sell or share it.
  • Promotions. A few spots on the site promote our own games and music. They’re ordinary links, with no ad network and no tracking.

Ads on this website

This website uses Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google’s Ad Settings.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.
  • How Google uses information from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you’re in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, you’re asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change that choice at any time from the privacy link on the page.

Children

This website and our games aren’t directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included.
