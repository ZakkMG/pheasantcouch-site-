Pew Pew Pest Control

Controls

  • W A S D move · mouse aims · hold click to shoot
  • Space jump · Shift sprint · F flick bugs off your boots
  • R ride a mount · E upgrades · M mute · Esc pause
  • Phones and tablets: left thumb moves, right thumb looks, and the PEW, JUMP and FLICK buttons do what they say.

The full list for the version you’re playing is on the title screen’s work order.

Saves

Progress saves automatically between jobs, on the device you’re playing on. The browser version keeps its save in that browser, so clearing the browser’s site data clears the save. Desktop and mobile builds each keep their own save.

The browser version runs slowly

The web build needs hardware acceleration. In Chrome or Edge, check that “Use graphics acceleration when available” is on in Settings, then reload. Closing other tabs that play video or 3D helps too.

Something’s broken

Email a note with what you were doing, what happened instead, and which version you’re on (it’s on the title screen). A screenshot or a short phone video helps a lot.
